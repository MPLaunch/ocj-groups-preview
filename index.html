The balcony fix

Leaking balcony? No jackhammers.

Most people with a leaking balcony expect the tiles to come up and a lot of mess. OCJ Groups' system goes straight over the top of your existing balcony. Nothing gets removed, and you're left with a clean new finish made with coloured recycled glass.

  • A finished surface, not a patch job
